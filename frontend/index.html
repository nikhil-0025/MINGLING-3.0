Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign up or create an account?
No! You receive a temporary session token the moment you land on the page. You can customize your nickname anytime.
Are my messages really deleted when the room expires?
Yes. Temporary messages are kept in Redis and ephemeral storage with automatic TTL expiration. Once expired, they are completely purged.
How does Saved Chats work?
If you explicitly click "Save Conversation", a snapshot is preserved in MongoDB under your session token so you can revisit or export it later.
